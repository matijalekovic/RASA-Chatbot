Nikola Tesla International Airport – Wayfinding Signage Design
Belgrade, Serbia
Enhancing clarity and orientation through a high-visibility wayfinding system
aligned with international airport standards.
Project Overview
In 2019, 1PAX was commissioned by Belgrade Airport to design the wayfinding
signage system for Nikola Tesla International Airport, a major regional hub serving
up to 15 million passengers annually. The project covered approximately 92,000 m²
of terminal space and focused on improving passenger orientation, gate visibility,
and overall legibility within a complex and evolving airport environment.
Key Challenge
The airport required a clear and consistent wayfinding strategy capable of guiding
high passenger volumes efficiently while adapting to operational changes and
terminal growth. The challenge was to enhance visibility and comprehension without
overloading the visual environment, ensuring that signage remained intuitive,
readable, and aligned with international concession and operational standards.
Our Approach
1PAX developed the wayfinding project using Vinci Airports Concessions charts as a
reference framework, ensuring consistency with global best practices. The system
emphasized improved gate visibility through carefully positioned signage boards and
totems. Backlit elements were integrated to enhance readability in varying lighting

conditions, reinforcing visual hierarchy and supporting effortless navigation across
key passenger decision points.
Value Delivered
The new wayfinding system significantly improved passenger orientation and
movement efficiency throughout the terminal. By combining clear graphic logic with
high-visibility, backlit signage, the project delivered a robust and adaptable
navigation framework. The result supports smoother passenger flows, operational
efficiency, and a more confident travel experience at Nikola Tesla International
Airport.
