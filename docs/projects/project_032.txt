Cabo Verde – Assistance for the Concession of Seven Airports
Cabo Verde, Macaronesia Ecoregion

Defining a strategic, multi-airport vision to strengthen connectivity and
support sustainable island development.
Project Overview
In 2019, 1PAX supported Vinci Airports in the successful concession process for the
management and development of seven airports across the Cabo Verde
archipelago: Sal, Santiago, Boa Vista, São Vicente, Fogo, São Nicolau, and Maio.
The assignment focused on the preparation of technical documentation for the
concession and extended into Phase 1B planning in 2025. The network includes
both international and domestic airports, each embedded within distinct geographic,
economic, and tourism-driven contexts.
Key Challenge
The primary challenge lay in addressing the diversity and dispersion of the airport
network while ensuring a coherent, long-term vision. Each airport presents unique
operational conditions—ranging from proximity to beaches and tourism resorts to
constraints linked to visibility, instrument flight procedures, and airspace
management. The strategy needed to enhance safety, capacity, and passenger
experience while remaining adaptable to the specific realities of each island.
Our Approach
1PAX contributed to the development of comprehensive technical documentation
supporting the concession process, combining strategic analysis with architectural
and planning expertise. For each airport, the team proposed a preliminary
masterplan tailored to local conditions, addressing capacity assessment, operational
constraints, and phased development scenarios. The approach emphasized
scalability, resilience, and clarity, ensuring that investments could be aligned with
traffic evolution and tourism growth while improving connectivity across the
archipelago.
Value Delivered
The project provided Vinci Airports with a robust, structured foundation for the
successful concession of Cabo Verde’s airport network. Through targeted
masterplanning, capacity evaluation, and phasing strategies, 1PAX helped define a
clear roadmap for coordinated development across seven islands. The outcome
supports improved safety, enhanced passenger experience, and sustainable growth,
positioning Cabo Verde’s airports as key enablers of regional connectivity and
economic development.
