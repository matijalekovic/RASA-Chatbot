Félix Eboué Cayenne Airport – Office Buildings
French Guiana
Delivering functional, purpose-built work environments integrated within a
complex airport ecosystem.
Project Overview
In 2023, as part of the broader renovation and expansion of Félix Eboué Cayenne
Airport, 1PAX was commissioned by EDEIS COLAS to design a series of dedicated
office and operational buildings. Developed in collaboration with Ingerop, the scope
included corporate facilities for Air Guyane and a specialized building for CNES,
integrating administrative, operational, and security-related functions within the
airport precinct.
Key Challenge
The project required accommodating highly specific operational needs within a
secure and constrained airport environment. The challenge was to deliver efficient,
clearly organized workspaces that support daily operations, security protocols, and
staff well-being, while ensuring seamless integration with airside activities and
adjacent infrastructure such as hangars and controlled access zones.
Our Approach
1PAX developed tailored architectural solutions for each entity. The CNES/VIP
building was designed to incorporate PIF control areas, reception spaces, passport
control, and sanitary facilities, ensuring clear separation between public, controlled,
and operational zones.
For Air Guyane, the offices located adjacent to the airline’s hangar were conceived
as a fully functional operational hub, integrating workshops, locker rooms, meeting
rooms, rest areas, and administrative offices. Spatial organization prioritized
efficiency, clarity of circulation, and direct adjacency to operational facilities,
supporting the airline’s daily workflows.
Value Delivered
The project delivered robust, fit-for-purpose office environments that respond
precisely to the operational realities of airport-based organizations. By aligning
spatial design with functional requirements, security needs, and staff comfort, 1PAX
contributed to improving operational efficiency and working conditions within Félix

Eboué Cayenne Airport, reinforcing its role as a well-coordinated and future-ready
aviation platform.
Headquarters of Qatar Railways
Doha, Qatar
Client: SETEC TPI/SIEMENS.
Architecture: 1PAX (as 1M2)
Area: 15 000M²
A contemporary civic workplace that embodies the identity and ambition of a national
mobility institution.
Project Overview →
Conceived as a competition entry for the headquarters of Qatar Railways in Doha,
this proposal reimagines the corporate workplace as an open, integrated, and
future-oriented urban ensemble. Designed by 1PAX (as 1M2) for SETEC TPI and
Siemens, the project consolidates offices, parking, and public space into a coherent
architectural composition embedded within the civic fabric.
Beyond accommodating administrative functions, the headquarters acts as a
public-facing institution—representing the operational excellence and
forward-looking vision of Qatar’s rail network. The design balances corporate rigor
with spatial generosity, reinforcing the organization’s role as a driver of national
connectivity and sustainable mobility.
Key Challenge →
The challenge was to reconcile operational precision and security requirements with
openness, accessibility, and urban integration. The headquarters needed to function
as an efficient administrative machine while projecting transparency and civic
presence—avoiding the isolation often associated with large institutional buildings.
Our Approach →
At 1PAX, every mobility project begins with people and experience . The
architectural concept is structured around clarity—clear circulation, legible spatial
organization, and intuitive movement. Office environments are designed to foster
collaboration, well-being, and adaptability, anticipating the evolution of workplace
culture and technology.
The ensemble is organized to create a dialogue between built form and public realm.
Carefully framed open spaces and shaded pedestrian areas soften the transition
between infrastructure and city, while parking and service areas are integrated
seamlessly into the overall composition.

Sustainability principles inform both form and performance. Passive strategies,
efficient envelopes, and resource-conscious systems reflect our commitment to
embedding life-cycle thinking and environmental responsibility into every project .
The result is an architecture that is disciplined yet welcoming—robust in operation,
refined in expression.
Value Delivered →
The proposal establishes a strong institutional identity for Qatar Railways—one that
communicates innovation, reliability, and civic responsibility. It creates an efficient
workplace environment that enhances productivity and employee well-being while
strengthening the organization’s public presence.
By integrating architecture, landscape, and infrastructure into a unified urban
gesture, the headquarters becomes more than an office building: it becomes a
symbol of national mobility—transparent, future-ready, and rooted in the city it
serves.
Greyfoot
Paris, France
Architecture: HERAULT ARNOD
Urbanism: STUDIO 1M2
Surface: 17.400 m2 Height: 40M
A metropolitan gateway that transforms a Parisian “porte” into a vibrant, mixed-use
urban catalyst.
Project Overview →
Greyfoot is a mixed-use development located at a strategic Parisian “porte,”
conceived as a catalyst for urban renewal and metropolitan continuity. Awarded First
Prize in competition, the project integrates offices, co-working spaces, housing, and
retail into a cohesive and future-ready urban ensemble. Developed for Unibail, the
scheme combines refurbishment and new construction to redefine the entrance to
Espace Champerret.
Led by Studio 1M2, the urban strategy strengthens connections between fragmented
city fabrics, transforming a transitional threshold into an active and recognizable
destination. Greyfoot positions itself not as an isolated building, but as a piece of
city—dense, permeable, and open to its surroundings.

Key Challenge →
The primary challenge was to reconcile multiple programs and scales within a
complex urban edge condition. As a “Paris door,” the site demanded both
metropolitan presence and local sensitivity. The project needed to bridge existing
neighborhoods, enhance pedestrian continuity, and integrate new functions without
disrupting the established identity of the area.
Our Approach →
At 1PAX, we understand architecture as a dialogue between movement, place, and
people . The masterplan articulates volumes to create urban porosity, allowing public
flows to traverse the site while structuring clear hierarchies between public,
semi-public, and private realms.
Refurbishment and new construction are carefully balanced: existing structures are
reinterpreted to extend their life cycle, while new volumes introduce a strong
architectural identity that signals arrival. The mixed-use composition promotes
vertical and horizontal diversity—retail animates the ground plane, workplaces foster
innovation and collaboration, and housing introduces permanence and daily life.
Sustainability and long-term adaptability guide the design. Life-cycle thinking informs
material choices and structural strategies, reinforcing our commitment to responsible
urban transformation . Landscaped terraces, shaded passages, and integrated green
elements soften the metropolitan scale and enhance environmental comfort.
Value Delivered →
Greyfoot redefines a Parisian gateway as a dynamic urban node—efficient,
inclusive, and resilient. The project strengthens urban continuity, generates
economic vitality, and creates a layered public realm that encourages interaction and
community life.
By combining strategic urbanism with architectural clarity, the proposal delivers a
mixed-use environment that is both iconic and contextual—an enduring metropolitan
presence that contributes meaningfully to the evolving identity of Paris.
