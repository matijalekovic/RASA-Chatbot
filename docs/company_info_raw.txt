Our Studio / About 1PAX
Opción larga:
1PAX is a forward-thinking architectural and innovation studio dedicated to reshaping the future of mobility and the way people experience the world. Our name—1PAX, meaning "one passenger"—reflects our core philosophy: every journey begins with a single person, and every design decision must honor the human experience at its heart.
Founded in 2016 by Mabel Miranda, 1PAX was born with a clear mission: to design with purpose, transform the way people and communities move, and promote a more sustainable, seamless relationship between people and their environments.
We are an interdisciplinary firm uniting architects, planners, urbanists, interior and graphic designers, innovators, and engineers. Together, we collaborate with mission-driven clients worldwide to shape spaces and systems that flow effortlessly, celebrate culture, and anticipate the needs of tomorrow.
With a global presence and a commitment to redefining mobility, we craft solutions at the intersection of architecture, technology, and environmental responsibility. Our expertise spans airport design, masterplanning, urban and regional mobility networks, eVTOL and heliport facilities, and patented innovations that push the boundaries of what mobility can become.
At 1PAX, we believe in a human-centered architecture—one where mobility is intuitive, environments are healthy, and design creates well-being and positive emotion. We design transportation infrastructure that is efficient, functional, inspiring, and truly sustainable.
Innovation is both our method and our ambition. We embrace challenges as opportunities to rethink, improve, and elevate the systems that connect our world.
For us, designing the present is shaping the future—and we are committed to contributing to a smarter, greener, and more resilient tomorrow.
We design for people—creating experiences that inspire, endure, and contribute meaningfully to the future of mobility and beyond.
Opción corta:
1PAX is a studio shaped by movement—an architectural practice dedicated to reimagining how people travel, connect, and experience the world. Our name, 1PAX, reminds us that every great journey begins with one passenger, one story, one human need.
Founded in 2016 by Mabel Miranda, we design to transform: to make mobility gentler, cities more connected, and our relationship with the environment more harmonious. Our interdisciplinary team blends architecture, planning, design, engineering, and innovation into a single creative momentum.
From airports and urban mobility networks to eVTOL terminals and visionary infrastructures, we work at the meeting point of architecture, technology, sustainability, and culture—crafting solutions that feel effortless and resonate with purpose.
At 1PAX, we design for what comes next. We create spaces that support well-being, encourage connection, and inspire a future where traveling feels natural, nurturing, and full of possibility. Innovation is our method

Our Design Approach
Human-Centered Design
Every project begins with a deep understanding of user needs, allowing us to design the best possible experience. We embrace simplicity that removes friction and increases accessibility. And we pursue beauty—because it invites interaction, sparks emotion, and leaves a lasting impression.
Sustainable by Nature
Society and the environment are at the heart of our process. We solve problems by inventing or integrating solutions that reduce carbon impact—prioritizing local materials, renewable energy, and intelligent resource use. Sustainability isn't an add-on; it's a principle we live by.
Designing for the Future
We design with tomorrow in mind. From buildings to experiences to products, we approach each challenge as researchers and innovators. We anticipate how expectations will evolve and help our clients stay ahead of what's next.
Architecture that Inspires
We design with a deep sense of place. Each project becomes a dialogue between culture, environment, and the human experience—spaces that invite interaction and leave behind stories that linger.
Functional creativity
We foster creativity, but every solution we propose is first and foremost functional and tailored to each client's situation. Our creativity is expressed not only in design, but also in how we achieve efficiency, sustainability, and an optimal user experience
Urbanism That Improves Lives
 At 1PAX, regenerative urbanism is a core part of our methodology. We approach the city as a living ecosystem, designing beyond sustainability to actively restore environmental systems, strengthen social dynamics, and generate long-term economic value. Through context-driven, human-centered design and circular use of resources, we transform urban development into a resilient framework that regenerates places, enhances quality of life, and creates lasting public value.

Our founder
Mabel Miranda: A Story of Courage and Purpose
The story of 1PAX is inseparable from the journey of its founder, Mabel Miranda—a journey shaped by resilience, curiosity, and an unwavering desire to contribute to society. Born in a developing country, Mabel transformed what others might have seen as limitations into pathways for growth, learning, and impact. Her early experiences instilled in her a deep sense of purpose and the conviction that design can be a powerful tool for positive change.
Driven by this belief, she sought out new cultures and immersed herself in the world, building a global perspective that today defines the spirit of 1PAX. Her path stands as an inspiration not just for women, minorities, or students, but for anyone who dreams of building a better future. Mabel's story shows that no barrier—social, economic, or geographical—can stop those who are determined to make a meaningful difference.
