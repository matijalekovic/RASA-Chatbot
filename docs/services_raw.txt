1PAX — Services Offering
========================

1. AIRPORTS & RAILSTATIONS
---------------------------
Functional planning, envelope and interior design.
Flow organisation, aircraft stands layout, BHS (baggage handling system) feasibility.
Fire safety compartmentation.
Train and metro stations, circulation sizing, multimodal connections, depot design.

Key capabilities:
- Terminal functional programming and capacity analysis
- Passenger flow simulation and peak-hour modelling
- Airside and landside masterplanning
- Baggage handling system feasibility and layout
- Fire safety strategy and compartmentation
- Multimodal hub design (air + rail + metro + bus + road)
- Check-in, security, boarding gate, and lounge sizing
- Greenfield airport design and brownfield terminal retrofit
- Train/metro station design and depot layout
- Circulation sizing and wayfinding strategy

2. URBANISM & MASTERPLAN
-------------------------
Development strategy, road access and parking planning.
Urban planning, airport cities.
Aircraft circulation study, vehicle turn simulation, ancillary buildings.
Traffic forecast, construction phasing, airport security review.

Key capabilities:
- Airport and transport hub masterplanning
- Airport city / aerotropolis concept and development strategy
- Road access, parking strategy, and kerb-front planning
- Aircraft taxiway/apron circulation study
- Vehicle swept-path and turn simulation
- Traffic forecast and modelling
- Construction phasing and interim operations planning
- Airport security perimeter review
- Urban regeneration around mobility infrastructure
- Mixed-use development strategy

3. INNOVATION & PATENTS
------------------------
Innovative cart design, modular airport furniture, licensing.
Passenger experience optimisation, commercialising patents.

Key capabilities:
- Patented luggage cart and trolley system design
- Modular, adaptable airport furniture systems
- Patent licensing to airport operators and retailers
- Passenger experience optimisation consulting
- Commercial development and roll-out of proprietary innovations
- Design research and prototype development

4. FUTURE MOBILITY
-------------------
Vertiport design, eco-friendly principles, urban mobility hubs.
Carbon neutrality, passenger-centric design, connected smart cities.
Collaborative innovation, MaaS (Mobility as a Service).

Key capabilities:
- Vertiport design for eVTOL and advanced air mobility (AAM)
- Heliport and urban air mobility facility planning
- Urban mobility hub integration (air + road + public transit)
- Carbon-neutral and net-zero mobility infrastructure
- MaaS (Mobility as a Service) planning and integration
- Smart city connectivity and digital infrastructure advisory
- Passenger-centric design for next-generation mobility
- Collaborative innovation with manufacturers and operators
- AAM feasibility studies and site selection

5. CONTROL TOWERS & ANCILLARY BUILDINGS
-----------------------------------------
Site masterplan and site selection, visibility requirements.
Control room height and fit-out, technical block layout, carpark design, fire safety.

Key capabilities:
- Air traffic control tower (ATCT) site selection and masterplan

- Technical block / equipment room layout
- Carpark and ancillary facilities design

Sizing and design brief, traffic forecast, work packages.
