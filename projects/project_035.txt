Félix Eboué Cayenne Airport – Masterplan
French Guiana
A strategic masterplan to enhance passenger experience, strengthen safety,
and unlock long-term airport and territorial development.
Project Overview
In 2023, 1PAX was commissioned by EDEIS COLAS to develop a comprehensive
masterplan for Félix Eboué Cayenne Airport, in collaboration with Ingerop. The study
addressed the airport as both an operational infrastructure and a driver of regional
development, with the objective of improving passenger experience while increasing
security levels and expanding aircraft apron capacity. The masterplan provides a
structured, long-term vision to guide phased development and coordinated
decision-making.
Key Challenge
The airport faced multiple, interrelated challenges: limited operational capacity on the
apron, safety concerns linked to the proximity of sensitive functions, and growing
pressure to accommodate new aviation and non-aviation activities. Any intervention
needed to improve performance without disrupting existing services, while also
anticipating future growth and diversification of the airport’s role within the region.
Our Approach
Following an extensive diagnostic and spatial analysis, 1PAX developed a
masterplan that reorganizes key airport functions to improve safety, efficiency, and
clarity. The strategy included proposals for new hangars designed to increase
capacity without impacting existing parking services, as well as the relocation of Air
Guyane facilities and the fuel depot to enhance public safety and operational logic.
The plan also expanded cargo parking areas to support logistics growth and
addressed landside development through the structuring of an adjacent economic
activity zone. This zone includes proposals for complementary programs such as a
hotel and a business center, reinforcing the airport’s role as an economic catalyst
beyond aviation operations.
Value Delivered
The masterplan provides Félix Eboué Cayenne Airport with a coherent and
forward-looking framework for development. By aligning passenger experience,
operational efficiency, safety, and economic diversification, the strategy supports
sustainable growth while minimizing risk and disruption. The result is a clear

roadmap that positions the airport as a resilient, multifunctional platform serving both
regional mobility and broader territorial development objectives.
Qatar Railways – Doha West Metro Depot Masterplan
Doha, U.A.E.
Client: SETEC BATIMENT/SIEMENS/BESIX/TSO
Designers/Urbanists: GracielaTorre, 1PAX (as 1M2)
Architecture: 1PAX (as 1M2), Jean Luc Chapel
Area: 157 216,72 m2
Transforming critical rail infrastructure into an efficient, human-centered urban oasis.
Project Overview →
Conceived as the masterplan for the operational heart of Qatar Railways, the Doha
West Metro Depot establishes a comprehensive framework that integrates depot and
maintenance facilities, stabling yards, control and training centers, power and water
plants, and parking within a single, coherent vision. The project goes beyond
technical coordination, redefining large-scale infrastructure as an integral and
articulated piece of the city. Anchored by its relationship to the Qatar Railways
headquarters, the masterplan introduces landscaped public spaces conceived as an
oasis—where operational performance and urban presence coexist.
Key Challenge →
The primary challenge lay in orchestrating a vast and highly technical
program—defined by strict operational, security, and logistical requirements—while
ensuring clarity of movement, long-term flexibility, and a dignified civic interface. The
depot needed to function as a resilient and efficient backbone for the metro system,
yet avoid becoming an isolated industrial enclave within the urban fabric.
Our Approach →
At 1PAX, mobility infrastructure is more than engineering—it is a spatial experience
shaped around people and purpose . The masterplan is structured through a clear
zoning logic that separates and optimizes operational flows—trains, maintenance,
staff, utilities, and visitors—while maintaining intuitive internal circulation.
A layered landscape strategy mediates between heavy infrastructure and the city.
Shaded courtyards, planted buffers, and water-conscious design principles introduce
environmental comfort and reduce heat gain, aligning with our commitment to embed
sustainability and life-cycle thinking into every project . The oasis concept becomes
both environmental infrastructure and social connector, reinforcing the identity of the

headquarters and offering moments of relief within a demanding technical
environment.
Functional creativity guides the architectural language: rational, durable, and
adaptable structures that support evolving rail technologies while preserving
operational efficiency. The result is a masterplan designed not only for today’s
performance metrics, but for tomorrow’s mobility ecosystems.
Value Delivered →
The Doha West Metro Depot transforms complex rail operations into a coherent,
future-ready system. It enhances operational efficiency, ensures seamless
coordination between infrastructure components, and strengthens the institutional
presence of Qatar Railways.
By integrating landscape, sustainability, and human-scale spatial strategies, the
project elevates infrastructure into civic architecture—an efficient urban asset that
supports resilience, well-being, and long-term adaptability. It stands as a model for
how large-scale mobility facilities can contribute meaningfully to the city they serve,
shaping a smarter and more sustainable urban future.
