Project Overview
In 2024, 1PAX was commissioned by Marseille Provence Airport to provide
architectural assistance and consulting services for the implementation of new
commercial catering facilities at Terminal 1. Working in collaboration with KAIRN
Ingénierie and within the architectural vision defined by Foster + Partners as Airport
Architect, the mission focused on programming, technical coordination, and strategic
support for a key component of the terminal’s extension and modernization.
Key Challenge
The development of future catering facilities required a clear and anticipatory
framework capable of aligning architectural intent, operational needs, and
commercial performance. The challenge was to define precise technical
requirements and spatial principles that could support diverse food and beverage
concepts, while remaining fully compatible with the terminal’s expansion, passenger
flows, and airport-wide operational constraints.
Our Approach
1PAX led a programming and consulting mission centered on the definition of
technical specifications for the catering areas within Terminal 1. The team translated
operational objectives into clear architectural and technical guidelines, addressing

layouts, servicing requirements, interfaces with public areas, and back-of-house
logistics. In parallel, 1PAX supported the preparation of tender documentation for
catering operators, ensuring that future concessions would align with the airport’s
quality standards, functional expectations, and long-term development strategy.
Value Delivered
The assignment provided Marseille Provence Airport with a structured, actionable
foundation for the successful implementation of its future catering facilities. By
clarifying requirements and aligning stakeholders early in the process, the project
reduced delivery risks, supported efficient procurement, and ensured coherence
between commercial ambitions and architectural design. The outcome contributes to
enhancing passenger experience and strengthening the terminal’s commercial
attractiveness within a major international gateway.
