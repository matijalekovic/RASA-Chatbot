Delivering a compact, high-performance hangar that supports fleet growth

Project Overview
In 2023, 1PAX was commissioned by EDEIS COLAS, in collaboration with Ingerop,
to design a new aircraft hangar for Air Guyane at Félix Eboué Cayenne Airport. The
project responds to the airline’s growing operational needs, providing additional

space to accommodate an expanding fleet while integrating seamlessly into the
existing airport infrastructure.
Key Challenge
The primary challenge was the site constraint: the designated area for the new
hangar was partially occupied by an active cargo parking zone critical to airport
operations. The project required a solution that could increase aircraft
accommodation capacity without compromising existing logistics functions or
reducing parking availability.
Our Approach
1PAX developed an innovative spatial strategy that optimized land use and carefully
balanced operational priorities. The hangar layout was designed to maximize internal
efficiency and aircraft maneuverability while preserving the full functionality of the
adjacent cargo parking area. Architectural and technical decisions were guided by
clarity of circulation, ease of maintenance, and direct integration with Air Guyane’s
operational workflows.
Value Delivered
The resulting hangar provides Air Guyane with a robust, future-ready facility that
supports fleet expansion without disrupting airport logistics. Its efficient layout,
pragmatic design, and seamless integration into the surrounding infrastructure
ensure long-term operational performance. The project strengthens Félix Eboué
Cayenne Airport’s capacity to support local aviation growth while maintaining a high
level of functional coherence across the airside environment.
