Kigali New Passenger Terminal Building – Consultation for Redesign &
Construction
Kigali, Rwanda
Optimizing design performance through targeted value engineering to deliver
a resilient, climate-responsive airport terminal.
Project Overview
In 2021, 1PAX was engaged to provide design assistance and value engineering
services for the new passenger terminal building at Bugesera International Airport,
Kigali. Commissioned by the joint venture led by Vinci Construction Grands Projets
in collaboration with Qatar Airways Investments, the assignment focused on
reviewing and refining key architectural and environmental aspects of a major new
airport infrastructure designed to accommodate up to 12 million passengers
annually.
Key Challenge
The project faced a series of design-related challenges associated with

constructability, environmental performance, and long-term operational efficiency.
Critical elements—such as the roof system, terminal canopies, and environmental
strategies—required reassessment to mitigate technical risks, improve climatic
responsiveness, and ensure that design ambition translated into a robust, buildable
solution suited to local conditions.
Our Approach
1PAX worked closely with the project stakeholders to identify potential design
inefficiencies and propose targeted improvements. The consultancy focused on
value engineering measures including adjustments to the direction of standing
seams, the removal of skylights to improve thermal control, and strategies to
optimize solar heat gains. Each recommendation balanced architectural intent with
performance, constructability, and lifecycle considerations, reinforcing a
human-centered and climate-aware design approach.
Value Delivered
Through strategic redesign and collaborative value engineering, 1PAX contributed to
enhancing the terminal’s functional efficiency, environmental performance, and user
comfort. The proposed solutions supported a more sustainable and resilient building
envelope while reducing technical complexity and long-term operational risks. The
outcome strengthens Bugesera International Airport’s capacity to serve as a
future-oriented gateway for Rwanda, aligned with international standards and
regional ambitions.
