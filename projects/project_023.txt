Tocumen International Airport – Fire Safety Strategy Review
Tocumen, Panama
Strengthening life-safety systems to support the safe expansion of Central
America’s primary aviation gateway.
Project Overview
In 2019, 1PAX was commissioned to contribute to the fire safety strategy review for
the expansion of Tocumen International Airport, Panama’s main international hub
and a critical infrastructure for regional connectivity. The project formed part of a
large-scale development led by Aeropuerto de Tocumen and EGIS, with architectural
design by Foster + Partners and ORAT services by EGIS. Designed to
accommodate growing passenger volumes and reinforce Panama’s role as a
regional and international gateway, the new infrastructure required rigorous
alignment between design intent, operational readiness, and safety compliance.
Key Challenge
The expansion introduced increased spatial complexity, higher passenger capacity,

and new operational scenarios that demanded a robust and coordinated fire safety
and evacuation strategy. Ensuring compliance with international standards while
translating technical requirements into practical, airport-wide operational protocols
was essential to guarantee passenger safety, staff preparedness, and seamless
day-to-day operations.
Our Approach
1PAX conducted an on-site visit to Tocumen International Airport, working in close
coordination with airport authorities and key stakeholders. The team reviewed both
existing and newly developed fire safety reports prepared under NFPA 101
standards, assessing their alignment with the expanded terminal configuration and
operational conditions. Building on this analysis, 1PAX contributed to the drafting of
the Airport Evacuation Plan and developed clear safety protocols tailored for
implementation by the airport’s employee brigades, bridging regulatory compliance
with operational clarity.
Value Delivered
The assignment strengthened the airport’s overall safety framework by ensuring
coherence between design, regulations, and operational practice. Through updated
evacuation strategies and actionable protocols, the project enhanced emergency
preparedness, reinforced operational efficiency, and supported the safe delivery of a
major international airport expansion. The outcome contributes directly to Tocumen
International Airport’s resilience, reliability, and long-term capacity to serve as a
leading aviation hub in Central America.
