Compact · Scalable · Sustainable
Ecoport is a patented multimodal vertiport system designed to
address one of today's most urgent challenges: how to deploy
advanced air mobility infrastructure quickly, sustainably and
efficiently, while ensuring passenger comfort and operational
performance.
Ecoport is not an abstract concept. 

It is a robust, modular and phased solution, ready to adapt to
real geographies, real regulations and real demand growth.

--- Page 7 ---
A new paradigm in mobility infrastructure
Today's mobility systems face growing challenges: territorial isolation, airport
congestion, inefficient passenger flows, and high CO₂ emissions. Addressing these
issues requires a fundamentally different approach to infrastructure.
At the same time, the emergence of advanced air mobility is opening new
possibilities for regional connectivity. Vertiports are beginning to appear around the
world, yet many current proposals rely on complex, site-specific solutions that slow
deployment, limit scalability, and restrict implementation across diverse geographic
conditions.
Ecoport introduces a compact, vertically organized, modular terminal, designed to:


Reduce walking distances and passenger processing times

Efficiently separate passenger flows

Integrate multiple transport modes within a single hub

Minimize environmental impact


All while remaining scalable, adaptable, and economically viable.

Connecting people where distance once divided
Expanding vertiport networks with our ecoport solution has the potential to transform everyday life.
By dramatically shortening travel times, Ecoport enables faster access to healthcare, education,
commerce, and essential services, particularly in geographically complex regions such as islands,
mountainous territories, dense urban corridors, and remote rural areas.
What once required hours -or even days- of travel by road or water can be reduced to minutes
through low-altitude electric mobility. Beyond efficiency, this means greater social inclusion, stronger
regional integration, and improved quality of life for communities historically limited by distance and
infrastructure constraints.

Designed for efficient passenger flow
Ecoport is structured around clear, measurable circulation logic, aligned with
international standards (IATA, ADRM, ICAO).
Key principles:











Clear separation of flows (departures, arrivals, transfers)

Reduced walking distances

Independent vertical circulation cores

Decentralized boarding by level

Intuitive wayfinding and spatial legibility

The result is a terminal that reduces congestion, shortens processing times and
improves passenger comfort, even during peak demand.

Modular growth, phase by phase



Ecoport allows operators to start small and scale intelligently, using a 30 × 30 m modular
grid:


Phase 00 – Compact vertiport base for drone logistics and light eVTOL operations

Phase 01 – Expanded single-level eVTOL terminal

Phase 02 – Integration with regional aircraft or multimodal ground transport

Phase 03–04 – Vertical expansion with independent circulation cores and increased
gate capacity


Each phase builds on the previous one, ensuring continuity of operations and long-term
flexibility.

Multimodal by design


Ecoport goes beyond eVTOL.
 
It integrates air, land and water mobility into a single coherent hub:

eVTOL, helicopters and general aviation aircraft

Electric buses and ground transport

Electric boats and seaplanes


This multimodal approach ensures territorial connectivity, reduces travel times and
enables low-carbon mobility networks — from cities to remote regions.
