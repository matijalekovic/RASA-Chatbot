Rethinking passenger mobility in airports
Passenger expectations for comfort, accessibility, and convenience are rising, while
terminal space remains limited.
Traditional solutions rely on multiple pieces of equipment — carts, seating, and
strollers — creating clutter, inefficiencies, and operational complexity within airports.
 
PAX is not just another airport cart.
 
It is a patented all-in-one mobility solution designed to redefine how passengers
move, wait, rest, and interact within airports and mobility hubs.


Conceived from a passenger-centered perspective, PAX seamlessly integrates
cart, seat, stroller, and stacking system into a single, elegant and highly efficient
design.
One object. Multiple uses. Endless possibilities.

Passenger expectations of comfort, accessibility and efficiency continue to rise

By transforming waiting time into usable time, PAX contributes to a more

All-in-one. One simple gesture.



PAX's patented transformation system allows it to shift effortlessly between four configurations:


Seat – Providing resting points anywhere, anytime

Cart – Facilitating the transport of hand luggage or shopping

Stroller – Enabling the safe transport of children or small pets

Stack / Nest – Ensuring compact storage and space efficiency


This multi-functionality delivers maximum utility with a reduced footprint, both in use and in
storage areas.
